# My Film-Watching History: Am I a Sheep?

An interactive Tableau analysis of 578 films I watched between 2021 and 2025.

The project explores the factors that may influence my film choices, including IMDb ratings, familiar actors, commercial success, cinema going habits and representation.

## Questions explored

- Do IMDb ratings influence what I watch?
- How often do I watch films featuring actors I recognise?
- Do I favour commercially successful films?
- How have my cinema and streaming habits changed?
- Do my viewing habits reflect my stated values around representation?
- How international is my film taste compared with commercially successful cinema?

![IMDb ratings analysis](images/film_git_pic_2.png)

## Data

I combined my own viewing records from 2021–2025 with external data including:

- IMDb ratings
- Worldwide box office data
- UK cinema attendance data
- Academy Awards data
- USC Annenberg Inclusion Initiative data
- International film industry benchmarks

Full sources are included in the Tableau dashboard.

## Tableau techniques used

- Calculated fields
- FIXED Level of Detail (LOD) expressions
- Interactive filters
- Highlight actions
- Parameters / date filtering
- Data visualisation and dashboard design
- Integration of multiple external datasets

## Key findings

- My viewing is concentrated around films with IMDb ratings of 6–8.
- Around 60% of the films I watched featured at least one actor I recognised.
- My viewing does not strongly favour the highest-grossing blockbuster films.
- Streaming dominated my viewing for much of the period, although my viewing methods changed considerably over time.
- Films directed by women remained a relatively small share of my viewing.
- My viewing is considerably more concentrated on English-language countries than the overall commercial film market.

## About this project

I built this project while transitioning into data analysis after previously working as a secondary Mathematics teacher.

The aim was not only to build a Tableau dashboard, but to take a personal dataset, combine it with external benchmarks and use the results to answer a broader question about my own behaviour.
